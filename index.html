物語

Passion, choice and trust

A Singapore bakery since 1981, built on Japanese methods and one simple belief: good quality is good service.

Four Leaves opened its first shop on Nutmeg Road in 1981. Forty five years later we run a central factory and thirty outlets of our own across Singapore, alongside a family of sister bakeries.

Every stage is checked, from the wheat flour we buy from one of Japan's leading millers to the loaf on the shelf. We hold our kitchens to Japanese standards of food safety and hygiene, because the bread is only as good as the care behind it.

Who we are

Company profile

Since we were founded in 1981, Four Leaves has kept growing and expanded across Singapore. We began with a single shop on Nutmeg Road, and over the years grew into a company running its own factory and many outlets.

Besides the Four Leaves brand, we run St Leaven at Takashimaya, Epi d'Or at Raffles City, Yamazaki and others, and we have expanded to Kuala Lumpur and Penang as Four Leaves Malaysia.

We bake our breads and cakes with the latest Japanese production methods, strict quality control and a spirit of creativity, because we believe that a product of good quality is good service to our customers. As food safety matters more and more in Singapore, we apply Japanese quality assurance standards throughout, and even our wheat flour comes from one of Japan's leading millers.

As a leading company in Singapore's bakery industry, we keep working to build a creative culture in the country's food industry, and in baking above all.

These answers are for guidance. Where they differ from the online order terms or the membership terms, those terms apply.

Ordering online

This is my first time ordering online. Any tips?+
Order as a guest, or create a membership account. An account saves your details for next time, so you do not fill the same fields in again, and it earns points.
Do I have to create an account to place an order?+
No. Guest checkout is always there. Membership is optional.
How many days in advance do I need to order?+
At least two working days before your collection date, not counting the day you order, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. The daily cut off is 23:59.
How far ahead can I order?+
Up to 14 days before the collection date, inclusive of the collection date.
It is past the cut off time. Can I still order for tomorrow?+
No. Once the cut off has passed we cannot take the order. You are welcome to drop by any outlet and buy off the shelf instead. Do check with the outlet on what is in stock before making the trip.
Can I order by phone or email?+
No. Orders are placed online or in person at any Four Leaves outlet.
How do I know my order is confirmed?+
We email a confirmation to the address on your order once payment goes through. Orders with a declined or pending payment are not processed, so place a new order if that happens.
What do I show when I collect?+
Your confirmation on your phone. Our staff check the name on the order and the order number against it, so there is nothing to print.
How do I request candles?+
Ask the staff at the outlet when you collect, after you have checked out and paid.
I called and emailed but heard nothing back.+
Apologies for that. Our customer service hours are 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, for both phone and email.

Changes and cancellations

Can I cancel my order?+
Yes, in writing at least two working days before your collection date, not counting the collection date. A cancellation fee of S$2.00 applies, and the refund comes as a Four Leaves voucher.
Can I cancel within two days of collection?+
You can tell us, but there is no refund, because the cake is already being made.
What can I change on a confirmed order?+
Your collection outlet and your collection time. The collection date cannot be changed once the order is submitted.
How much notice do you need for a change?+
Two working days before your collection date, not counting the collection date itself.
How do I amend or remove items in my basket?+
Before checkout, open your basket, use the edit icon to change a quantity or a choice, and the delete icon to remove an item.
If I do not collect my order, will I be refunded?+
No. Uncollected orders are not refunded or exchanged.

Payment

What can I pay with?+
Visa or Mastercard, or eNETS QR. We do not accept other cards.
When is payment taken?+
When you submit the order. Prices include GST.
Is my card stored?+
No. Card details go to our payment processor to take the payment and are not retained by us.

Membership

What is Four Leaves membership?+
A free loyalty programme for customers who order online. You earn a point for every S$2 spent, and points redeem for e-vouchers to use on later online orders.
Is there a membership fee?+
No. Joining is free.
Why do I have to verify my email?+
To confirm the account belongs to you. Verification is sent to the address you registered with.
When do points appear?+
After the order has been collected. One point for every S$2 spent.
When do points expire?+
Points earned in a calendar year expire on 31 December of that year. We email a reminder a month before.
How many points for an e-voucher?+
60 points for a $5 voucher, 100 points for a $10 voucher, and 120 points for a $20 voucher.
How long does an e-voucher last?+
Until the last day of the eleventh month after the month you redeemed it. Redeem one on 20 December 2026 and it runs to 30 November 2027.
Can I use several e-vouchers on one order?+
Yes. Points are not awarded on the part paid with vouchers, only on the rest.
Will my account be deleted if I do not use it?+
If you do not order online at least once in two years, the account is deleted at the end of that period.
I have an account but no password.+
Use "Forgot password" and we will email you instructions. Check your junk folder if it does not arrive.
Can I change the email address on my account?+
Yes. You will be asked to verify the new address.
Where do I change my details?+
Sign in, open My account, then Account settings.

We would rather hear from you than not. This page explains where feedback goes, how long it takes, and what we will do about a product you are unhappy with.

Notice

All enquiries and issues need two days advance notice in writing.

Where to send it

Send feedback on any Four Leaves product through the form on our contact page, or by email to onlinesupport@fourleaves.com.sg.

If something urgent comes up on a weekend or a public holiday, raise it at any of our outlets. The staff there will pass the details and your contact information to the head office, and we will contact you on the next working day.

Products

Feedback about a product means bringing it back to the outlet you bought it from, or the nearest outlet to you. Please keep a reasonable amount of the product so that we can investigate.

  1. Our liability is limited to replacing the exact product, replacing it with one of equivalent value, or refunding it in Four Leaves vouchers.
  2. Claims on a faulty product must be made within two days of receiving it. After that we may not take the complaint further.
  3. We may ask for evidence, such as the item itself, a photograph or the invoice. Without it a claim may not be valid.
